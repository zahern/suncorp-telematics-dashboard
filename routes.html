Hottest roads

Predictions load with the plan.

Volume ranks roads by predicted Compass speeding in the bin. Surge ranks the same roads against their own 28-bin mean, so a road that is quiet for most of the evening and spikes now outranks one that is merely busy throughout. Lift baselines the same profile on the bins before kick-off only, which is where the arrival build-up lives, so a road that is already at its evening peak well before the whistle does not flatten the scale at kick-off. Roads carrying under 15% of the bin's busiest are dropped in both ratio views, because their ratio is mostly noise. In volume the bar is relative to the bin's leader; in surge and lift it grows right of centre above the road's baseline and left of it below, scaled to the biggest gap in the list. The sparkline is the road's own profile with the dashed line at its baseline and the dot at the clock. Click a row to locate it.

Post legend

Each row is one officer over the 28 bins of the fixture window; hatched cells are transit bins, which earn no coverage. The dashed line is the solved relocation route; the solid segment is the post held at the clock time shown. Positions are OSM segment midpoints a post was solved on, not GPS traces. The plan buttons switch between the MIP roster and the roving plan over the parking-verified pool; the squad slider re-solves nothing — it selects one already-solved squad size (P = 1..8) from the same MIP sweep, and the coverage curve under Fixture shows what each extra officer buys.
